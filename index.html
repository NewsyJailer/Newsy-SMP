About Newsy SMP

Welcome to Newsy SMP 💚, we're an SMP that uses different mechanics each season to allow different types of players to have their own fun.

💔 Lifesteal

Fight with others and steal their hearts.

💪🏾 Strength

Gain your strength by killing people.

💎 Bliss

Use your gem powers to defeat mobs and players!

🏆 Ranked

Climb the ranks to gain more hearts, bigger inventories and the most respect.

🌐 Zone Wars

The world shrinks until it's time to face off with your enemies.

» Server Rules

1. No discriminatory behavior

Any behavior like this will result in a ban from the server.

2. No sending NSFW or 18+ content

This will result in a ban and a report to Discord for sending 18+ content.

3. Don't spam ping anyone

The only time pinging staff is acceptable is when it's justified. — Results in Timeout.

4. Please don't self-promo, or promo in general unless in self-promo

Will result in your message being taken down and a warning.

5. Please show respect to all Staff

If respect is not shown, you will be kicked or timed out.

6. Don't make any threats at all

Results in a ban and a report to Discord.

7. Try to have a fun time in our server!

» SMP Rules

1. No cheating on the server

This can result in a punishment from a temporary ban to a permanent ban.

2. No Combat or Danger Logging

Results in a temporary ban (up to 72 hours).

3. The Max Team Size is SIX

If any team has more than six players, then that team will get timed out.

4. No Discrimination

If found guilty of this, you will be banned permanently & reported to Microsoft. We take discrimination very seriously.

5. No killing/farming Naked Players unless they have been on the server for more than 2 real-life days

24 Hour Ban.

6. Please respect our Staff Members

If you are found being rude to a staff member for no reason, you will get a temporary ban.

7. Please respect other people

You don't know what they could be going through. — Week Ban. (Trash talking another person is allowed.)

8. Enjoy your time here at Newsy SMP!
